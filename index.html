Iran Is Currently Facing a Devastating Crisis!

During recent nationwide demonstrations, more than 30,000 innocent people have been killed by the security forces of the Islamic regime, the ruling government of Iran. Protesters were targeted in the streets while demanding an end to the current regime. The uprisings began with merchants and shopkeepers going on strikes in Tehran in response to severe economic conditions, including inflation and currency collapse. Within a short time, these actions spread across the country, turning into widespread protests involving cities and towns throughout Iran.

Iran Is Bleeding

Protesters in the streets called for an end to the current regime and expressed support for Reza Pahlavi, the son of Iran’s former Shah, as a representative for a future transition. In response, the Islamic Republic carried out a severe and coordinated crackdown. Reports indicate that hospitals were attacked, wounded protesters were killed, and families were forced to pay to recover bodies. Despite these crimes, the Islamic Republic continues its brutality. Many have been arrested and remain missing. Numerous young people are at risk of execution. These abuses will not stop unless major world powers take meaningful action.

What Do Iranians Want from the World?

The goal is a FREE Iran with Reza Pahlavi as the leader of the transition towards a democratic future chosen by the people. The Iranians are calling for regime change and a clear path forward, and what they are asking from the world is simple: to BE their VOICE in this journey towards freedom.

When a Nation Goes Dark: Iran’s 2026 Internet Blackout

The 2026 Iran internet blackout has left millions disconnected from loved ones, reliable information, and basic digital life. Beyond a technical shutdown, it has become a humanitarian, economic, and political crisis affecting every part of society.
